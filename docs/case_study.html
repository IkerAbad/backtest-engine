Out-of-sample result The walk-forward strategy returned 69.1985% over 2018-11-21 to 2025-11-28, with a Sharpe ratio of 0.7980 and a maximum drawdown of -12.9362%. It underperforms the 187.5521% buy-and-hold return over the same dates.

What is measured

This is a real daily history of SPY, not synthetic data. The strategy is a long/flat moving-average rule. Every fold selects its window only on the training block, then runs the winning window on the following, non-overlapping test block. Costs are charged on every fill: 1 bps commission, 2 bps spread, and 100 bps slippage at 100% participation.

The source is Yahoo Finance's chart API. OHLC values are multiplied by Yahoo's adjusted close, so splits and distributions are reflected in the price series. Volume is unchanged. Yahoo data is used here for research; check the provider's terms before commercial use.

Walk-forward design

Training window: 504 bars. Test window: 252 bars. Step: 252 bars, so test windows are contiguous and never overlap. Grid: 20, 50, 100, 200. The winner is chosen by training Sharpe, then total return, then grid order. 7 folds fit in the available history.

The subtle strategy does not peek at tomorrow's price. It computes the mean and standard deviation of the whole sample and normalizes each close with them. In the masked replay it only sees bars up to the clock, so the first standard deviation is zero and it stays flat. The detector compares the two decision traces and reports the first difference.
